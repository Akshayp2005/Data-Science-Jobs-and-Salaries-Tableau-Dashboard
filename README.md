# Data-Science-Jobs-and-Salaries-Tableau-Dashboard
An interactive Tableau workbook analyzing global data science job salaries — covering experience level, employment type, company size/location, and top employee residences.


## Project Overview
This project explores a dataset of data science job salaries (ds_salaries.csv) to uncover trends in compensation across different roles, experience levels, employment types, and geographies.


## Files in this Repository

(pro.twb)	Tableau workbook file containing all worksheets and visualizations.

(ds_salaries.csv)	Source dataset used in the analysis.

(README.md)	Project documentation.


## Visualizations Included

Employment Type – Breakdown of full-time, part-time, contract, and freelance roles.

Experience Level – Salary distribution across entry, mid, senior, and executive levels.

Company by Size and Location – How company size and location relate to compensation.

Top 10 Employee Residence – Countries with the most represented employees in the dataset.


## Key Insights

Experience level skews senior: Senior-level (SE) employees make up 46.1% of all records, followed by Mid-level (35.1%), Entry-level (14.5%), and Executive (4.3%) — the workforce here is dominated by experienced professionals.

Full-time is the norm: 588 of 607 roles (96.9%) are full-time (FT); part-time, contract, and freelance combined make up just 3%.

Salary rises sharply with seniority: Average salary jumps from $61,643 (Entry) → $87,996 (Mid) → $138,617 (Senior) → $199,392 (Executive) — executives earn over 3x what entry-level employees earn.

The US dominates the dataset: 332 of 607 employees (55%) reside in the US, and 355 (58%) of companies are US-based — by far the largest single country, with the UK (44), India (30), and Canada (29) a distant second tier.

Data Scientist is the most common role: The top 3 job titles — Data Scientist (143), Data Engineer (132), and Data Analyst (97) — account for 62% of all records.

Larger companies pay more: Large companies average $119,243, medium companies $116,905, and small companies trail noticeably at $77,633.

Fully remote roles pay best on average: Fully remote (100%) roles average $122,457, versus $106,355 for fully on-site (0%) and just $80,823 for hybrid (50%) — hybrid arrangements are actually the lowest-paid on average in this dataset.

Salaries have climbed year over year: Average salary rose from $95,813 (2020) → $99,854 (2021) → $124,522 (2022), a ~30% increase over two years.

Wide overall salary range: Salaries in USD range from $2,859 to $600,000, with an overall average of $112,298 and median of $101,570 (the gap between mean and median suggests a small number of very high earners pulling the average up).
